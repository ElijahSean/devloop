# LEAD: judgement and communication

- **LEAD-1 Tie work to the goal.** State what success looks like and how it is measured. (Staff 3, 5)
- **LEAD-2 Be clear and opinionated in writing.** A specific wrong statement gets corrected, while a vague one survives. State what you are optimising for. (Staff 5)
- **LEAD-3 Plan for change.** Assume dependencies slip and directions change. Name the plan's assumptions. (Staff 5)
- **LEAD-4 Unblock by narrowing.** When stuck, say what is needed, reduce what others must do and offer the smallest decision to make. Do not keep trying silently. (Staff 6)
- **LEAD-5 Done means done.** Code complete is one milestone. Done means the requirement works, is verified and has been reported honestly, including what failed or was skipped. (Staff 6)
- **LEAD-6 Turn repeats into checks.** A lesson learned twice becomes an automated check or a rule, because habits built into the workflow outlast advice. (SEAG 7, 8; Staff 8)
- **LEAD-7 Calm and proportion.** Make problems smaller. Separate the urgent from the merely interesting, and keep unrelated improvements out of the change. (Staff 4, 7)
- **LEAD-8 Measure only what you would act on.** Trace each metric to a goal, and do not improve one dimension of quality by quietly spending another. (SEAG 7)
